## Notes

- Do not add CTAs inside the post body. Add the blog link as the first comment after posting — this is intentional to avoid LinkedIn algorithm penalty on outbound links in the post text.
- The `---` markers in each `.md` file delimit the post body. Copy only what is between them.
- Bold formatting (`**text**`) renders correctly when pasted into LinkedIn's editor.
- Line breaks between paragraphs are intentional — LinkedIn compresses excessive blank lines.
- Word counts: Company posts ~200–250 words. Founder posts ~350–450 words.
