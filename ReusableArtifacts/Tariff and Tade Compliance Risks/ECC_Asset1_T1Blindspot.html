Why Standard Tools Cannot Do This
1
No BOM graph below Tier 2. Supplier risk platforms map company-to-company relationships. They have no material nodes, no HS codes at depth, no composition data. The NdFeB powder at L5 is invisible by architecture.
2
No substantial transformation test. Proving Vietnamese origin requires: input HS code (L5), output HS code (L4), chapter change confirmed at a specific facility by trade records. Without the material graph, this computation is impossible.
3
No cross-assembly multiplier. NdFeB magnets appear across multiple assemblies in the same vehicle. One attestation resolves all of them simultaneously. Without the inverted tree, this resolution path is invisible.
4
No proactive discovery. Competing tariff tools calculate rates for products you submit. ECC finds the products that need submitting — across all BOM positions, all assemblies, all vehicle lines — before you know to ask.
The ECC Approach
ECC's material genealogy traces every component to its chemical precursor at L5 — where the HS code, geographic origin, and tariff regime are determined. Not the supplier location. The material inside the supplier's product.

When Panjiva trade records confirm a processing step that changes the HS chapter — from Chapter 28 (alloy powder) to Chapter 85 (permanent magnet) — ECC records the transformation evidence, calculates the revised tariff state, and generates the pre-drafted attestation request. The compliance team reviews and signs. The supplier confirms.
