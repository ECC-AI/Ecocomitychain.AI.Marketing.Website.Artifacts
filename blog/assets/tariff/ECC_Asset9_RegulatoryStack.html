Platform Capability Breakdown
What every regulation needs
  • Composition What substance or material is present at each level
  • Quantity Volume or mass per BOM position, per assembly
  • Origin Country and facility where material was produced or processed
  • Production route How it was processed — essential for carbon accounting and transformation determination
  • Attestation Is origin consistent with supplier declaration or certificate of origin?
  • Audit trail Can you prove all of the above to a regulator, with timestamped evidence?
What ECC provides
  • Material genealogy L1→L8 Composition data from material composition database, DoE/USGS/USLCI baseline
  • Quantity at every level qty_per_parent relationship at every graph node, propagated automatically
  • CPD origin field Critical Precursor Dependency origin + third-party trade monitoring platform records confirmation
  • Production route characterisation NREL USLCI + DoE Critical Materials Assessment for carbon and transformation evidence
  • ExplanationStep audit trail State transitions with timestamps, evidence sources, actor attribution — per node
  • Pre-generated compliance packages Evidence structured per regulatory instrument — not one generic export
What the compliance team does
  • Reviews ECC's starting point Pre-populated baseline from public data — not a blank questionnaire
  • Corrects supplier-specific data Where actual sourcing differs from ECC's baseline inference, suppliers update directly
  • Approves attestation requests Signs off on supplier attestations generated by ECC's workflow
  • Uploads formal certificates CO, SVHC disclosure, RoHS declaration — attached to the relevant graph node
  • Submits to regulators Uses ECC's audit trail as the exhibit in CBP, REACH, and battery regulation submissions
  • Monitors evolving exposure As regulations change, ECC re-scores from the same graph — no new data collection
