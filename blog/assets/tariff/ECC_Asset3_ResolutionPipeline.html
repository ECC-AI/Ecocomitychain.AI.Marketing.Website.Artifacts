01
The Reactive Discovery Problem
Tariff exposure is invisible until enforcement makes it visible. CBP's Automated Targeting System flags entries using pattern recognition trained on millions of prior cases. By the time a CF-28 arrives, the importer is already the subject of an investigation — not a routine enquiry.
02
The Visibility Gap
Standard supply chain platforms map supplier relationships to Tier 2–3. Tariff liability is determined at L4–L5 — the material chemistry level. This is where Chinese-origin content lives: inside German-assembled motors, inside Japanese-processed components, invisible on the customs entry.
03
The Response Paralysis
Once the CF-28 arrives, the OEM has three options: pay the retroactive duty, provide documentation they don't have, or argue for prior disclosure protection they cannot claim. Every option is expensive. None are good. All were avoidable.
