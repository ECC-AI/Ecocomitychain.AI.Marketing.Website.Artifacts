Why the constraint lives here
The Galfenol alloy requires a proprietary magnetic field alignment fixture during casting — a process tool held by one qualified supplier globally. Substitution requires:
01 New fixture fabrication — 8–10 weeks minimum
02 Magnetostrictive property validation — 6–8 weeks
03 OEM PPAP re-qualification — 8–12 weeks
TOOLING PRECEDENT
Land Rover / UPF Thomson (2001): chassis tooling transfer took 6–9 months despite Land Rover owning the dies. The bottleneck is always qualification, not transport.
