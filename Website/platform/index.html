Why both capabilities matter

Proactive intelligence doesn't stop when disruption starts

When an event occurs, your reactive response is only as good as the intelligence behind it. Companies that built proactive resilience do not start their ICA planning from scratch. The exposure map is already built. The risk priorities are already scored. The Resilience Copilot has context from day one.

And every lesson learned from navigating a disruption feeds back into proactive readiness. The two capabilities improve each other over time. That compounding is what separates a resilient supply chain from one that simply survived the last event.

Risk analytics identify the vulnerability weeks or months before it becomes a crisis. Prescriptive guidance reduces it while there is still time.

Copilot guides the team through every decision

Every response strengthens future readiness
