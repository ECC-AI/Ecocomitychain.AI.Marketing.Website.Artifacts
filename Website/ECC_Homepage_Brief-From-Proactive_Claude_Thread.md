# ECC Homepage — Thread Brief

---

## Company

**EcocomityChain.AI (ECC)** — value chain resilience platform. Pre-first-customer. Platform fully built and running.

**Positioning in one sentence:** ECC helps manufacturers anticipate supply chain disruptions before they become crises, and navigate them intelligently when they do.

**Two live platform segments:**
- **Proactive resilience** — risk intelligence + prescriptive guidance. Know what's coming before it arrives.
- **Reactive resilience** — ICA Intelligence flows + Resilience Copilot. Navigate disruption with a scientifically grounded strategy. Built first, fully live.

**Approved hero headline:** *"The supply chains that don't break aren't lucky. They're built differently."*
